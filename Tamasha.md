I recently watched tamasha movie, After a long time I felt a difference. Not just while watching the movie. Honestly, I watch movies very deeply, its weird but almost every movie I watch, I get small tears in my eyes just because it just feels so immersive in the movie. But this one was different, loved every bit of it and I guess Imtiaz Ali wrote this in a very generic yet specific way that almost everyone somehow relates with the flow of the movie. Everything that happens to Ved has happened to audience at some point in their lives. Our brain just starts mapping the emotional depth with our reality and it gets so deep that later we start feeling like Ved, for example by the end when Ved meets storyteller, I also had no idea what will or should happen in this story now, the story is not predictable anymore, now we ourself are rooting for something good to happen but we don't know what! 

Before watching this movie I had listened to `wat wat` song and it was good but on the surface level it looked like a normal *Indian pop song* and to be honest for me I was confused that how this song must be fitting in this kind of movie. But when you watch the movie, you just start enjoying the song because Ved is enjoying. This song felt totally different with Ved's perspective in the eyes.

The corporate culture:
Being in the corporate myself, I felt the void. He was doing good (crazy good) not in so sad way or even he was happy doing it. But the question wasn't about right or wrong, or happy or sad. It was about completeness, that once you have seen a perfect version of someone, you simply can't accept them in different identity. Tara felt that void, of course Ved was also feeling it but it was Tara who questioned his reality, and she was probably the first person in his life who did that other than himself.
