I am thinking about how people will or should use this idea of mine.
I have few thinkings, like, the canvas mode. Canvas mode can be helpful for teaching a topic or smaller things to people (students, general audience)
how people interact with the canvas or before that I wanna explain how I am imagining that people should interact with it is:
the canvas will be a playground where whatever discussion I am having with AI will be getting presented. In this mode, we will only show canvas and the subtitles, Its more of a conversation with visual help.
For example, if I am asking AI about the places that I wanna visit, or wanna plan my trip. Then it should first make the playground a template or empty vision board for me which will start getting filled conversation by conversation. So, if I wanna plan a trip, it should put a heading that says, winter trip for swayam. 
At left side, it will have a box of must things, ideas. These will be getting filled as per my conversation and only when AI gets to a point where it have all the data points to start actually showing me results, it should fill in the results like location, flight options, budget, things to do etc.
Future plan coming to my mind is like a whiteboard assisted by AI, where user can also do something and AI gets the context. 

This above was about canvas, now I wanna talk about other aspects. As, sandbox coding where if I don't have the respective tool for the user then it should ideally make a script, run it and then share the outcome. 

The other aspect is good memory, every chat should be fresh as new but as contextually aware are our brain such that it can retrieve any piece of relevant information at any time with absolute less latency. This here is a hard part I feel.

One more is the conversational UX, although there are companies like elevenlabs, smallest ai which are building conversational suite but even with 11labs I see that the fluency in conversation is not present. I want the system to be very flexible, like our brain, I don't always put my head deeply into all the things, few answers I just know so why to use extended thinking or add complexity to them. It should be simple ye ready to handle complex tasks. This too is the hard part.

I am not going into the language problem here yet but that's also another large and very important part of it.

More on convUX, not just the latency, but the fluency, way of speaking, interruption handling etc are also need to be taken care of.

Combining all of these will produce a much more powerful conversational ai agent complete suite for the intelligence. Where people can not just create a voice agent with customised tool but can also use this product in daily life. Imagine, all of the powerful functionalities just on your lips.

What I am envisioning is a future product which can be used by user or group of users together to discuss almost everything with confidence as like we do it with current chatbots but with their voice.
